About Me

I am a Research Associate in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, working under the supervision of Dr. Paul Tsang. My research connects Operations Research with Complex Systems. I use data, mathematical models, and optimization to support better decisions in logistics, supply chains, transportation, and networked systems.

Previously, I completed an M.Sc. in Industrial Engineering and Logistics Management in the Department of Data and Systems Engineering at The University of Hong Kong under the supervision of Prof. Jiayang Li. I also completed a B.Sc. in Systems Science in the School of Systems Science and a B.Ec. in Finance at Beijing Normal University under the supervision of Prof. Zengru Di.

I am working on:

Data-driven decision making
Logistics and supply chain management
Complex networks theory

Starting Spring 2027, I will join the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University as a Ph.D. student.
