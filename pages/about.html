About Me

I am a Research Associate in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering (ISE) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), working under the supervision of Dr. Paul Tsang. My research connects Operations Research with Complex Systems. I use data, mathematical models, and optimization to support better decisions in logistics, supply chains, transportation, and networked systems.

Previously, I obtained a M.Sc. (Eng.) from The University of Hong Kong (HKU) and a B.Sc. and a B.Ec. from Beijing Normal University (BNU).

My current research focuses on:

Data-driven decision making
Logistics and supply chain management
Complex network theory

Starting in Spring 2027, I will join the ISE at PolyU as a Ph.D. student.

If you share similar interests or would like to chat, feel free to reach out at haotiantimxie@gmail.com.
