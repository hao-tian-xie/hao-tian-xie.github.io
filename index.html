About

I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. I received my Master's degree from The University of Hong Kong in 2025 and my Bachelor's degree from Beijing Normal University in 2024.

My work connects Operations Research with Complex Systems. I study how data, mathematical models, and optimization can support better decisions in logistics, supply chains, transportation, and networked systems.

Current research interests:

  • Data-driven decision making
  • Logistics and supply chain management
  • Complex networks theory

I am actively seeking a Ph.D. position starting in Fall 2026 and welcome academic collaborations.
