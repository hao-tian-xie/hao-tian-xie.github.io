01 · About

Research at the edge of systems and decisions.

Building useful models for complex, interconnected real-world systems.

I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). I received my Master's degree from The University of Hong Kong (HKU) in 2025 and my Bachelor's degree from Beijing Normal University (BNU) in 2024.

My work sits at the intersection of Operations Research and Complex Systems. I am especially interested in data-driven decision making, logistics and supply chain management, and complex networks theory.
