About

I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). I received my Master's degree from The University of Hong Kong (HKU) in 2025 and my Bachelor's degree from Beijing Normal University (BNU) in 2024.

My work connects Operations Research with Complex Systems. I study how data, mathematical models, and optimization can support better decisions in logistics, supply chains, transportation, and networked systems.

My current research interests are:

  • Data-driven decision making
  • Logistics and supply chain management
  • Complex networks theory

Open to conversations. I am actively seeking a Ph.D. position starting in Fall 2026 and welcome academic collaborations. Please feel free to reach out by email.
